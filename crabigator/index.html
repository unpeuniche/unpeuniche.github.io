Crabigator - WaniKani Reverse Flashcards

A personal study tool that pulls unlocked WaniKani items and lets me practise writing kanji and vocabulary. All data are fetched from the official WaniKani API. API token is stored only in your browser’s local storage and is never sent anywhere.

AI disclosure: I used Claude Sonnet to help me develop this small application. Part of me is ashamed, part of me is happy with the result. Idk, I let you judge.
